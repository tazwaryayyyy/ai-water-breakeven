> AI has a water burden.
> AI can also enable interventions that save water elsewhere.
> Because both sides are uncertain, simulate many plausible scenarios and ask when, or whether, cumulative AI-enabled water savings exceed cumulative AI water use.

The real research repository currently describes itself as:

**AI Water Break-Even**
